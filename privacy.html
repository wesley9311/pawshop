Prelaunch privacy notice

The current preview does not submit inquiry, payment, address or order information to PawShop.

Data stored on your device

The preview may store language preference and a saved product list in your browser. This information stays on that device and can be removed by clearing site data.

Hosting and media

The site is served from PawShop's own server infrastructure on a self-managed cloud host, where product images and interface assets are served from the same origin as the site and no third-party content delivery network is required. A static preview mirror of the showcase is also published on GitHub Pages, which may load some product media from a third-party image host. These providers may receive standard technical request information such as IP address, browser type and requested URL.

Before sales open

This notice will be replaced with a complete policy identifying the business operator, contact channel, purposes of processing, service providers, retention periods and customer rights before any personal information is collected.
